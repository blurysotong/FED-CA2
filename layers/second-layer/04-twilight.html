04 / Second layer

200–1,000 m
Mesopelagic zone

Light becomes a trace

Twilight

The mesopelagic is the ocean's dim middle layer. A little sunlight reaches this far, but it is too weak for plants and algae to use for photosynthesis.

What changes here?

Depth
About 200 to 1,000 metres below the surface.
Sunlight
Faint blue light remains, but daytime feels like dusk.
Life
Many animals have large eyes or dark bodies to survive unseen.
