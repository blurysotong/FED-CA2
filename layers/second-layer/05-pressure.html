Not all snow is natural

Plastic
Marine
Snow

Microplastics can clump together, sink independently, or become trapped inside organic marine snow. Twilight-zone fish may mistake these particles for food, risking injury, breathing problems, and organ damage. The same plastic can travel back up the food chain.

The scale of the problem

171 trillion

Plastic pieces were estimated to be floating in the world's oceans in a 2023 study reported by BBC News.

Take action today

This damage is preventable.

Better coastal waste management and less single-use plastic can stop debris before it enters the ocean. Small choices on land protect animals hundreds of metres below the surface.
