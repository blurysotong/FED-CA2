Living light
Bioluminescence

A signal in the dark

Glow

Bioluminescence is light made by a living organism through a chemical reaction. In dark water, a tiny flash can attract prey, confuse a predator, or send a message to another animal.

Why make light?

Hunting
A glowing lure can draw curious prey within reach.
Defence
A sudden flash can startle predators or hide an animal's outline.
Communication
Patterns of light can help animals recognise or find one another.

Below the twilight zone, natural sunlight disappears completely.
