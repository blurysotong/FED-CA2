Built for pressure

Adapt to the deep

Deep-sea animals cannot escape the weight of the water above them. Instead, their bodies have changed over many generations to work in high pressure, low temperatures, and scarce food.

Survival toolkit

Soft bodies
Flexible tissue contains fewer air spaces that pressure can crush.
Slow energy use
A slow metabolism helps limited meals last for longer.
Stable cells
Special molecules help proteins keep their shape under intense pressure.
