Eerie creatures…

Creatures here produce their own bioluminescent light. The most known example you’ve probably heard of is the

Anglerfish.

Famous for the glowing rod protruding from its forehead, it uses bioluminescence to lure confused prey through pitch darkness. Females of some species are massive compared with the males, which fuse to them as tiny parasites just to ensure they can mate.

A really unique species here is the

Atolla jellyfish.

It uses a brilliant defense system called a “burglar alarm.” When grabbed, it pinwheels bright flashing lights—not to scare its attacker, but to call in a larger predator to eat whatever is attacking the jellyfish.
