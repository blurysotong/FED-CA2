Food from above

Marine snow

Tiny pieces of organic matter drift down from brighter water. Together they look like snow, and they carry precious food to animals living far below the sunlit surface.

A slow food delivery

What it is
Bits of plankton, waste, mucus, and other organic material.
Where it goes
Flakes sink slowly through the water, sometimes reaching the seafloor.
Why it matters
It feeds deep-ocean life where plants cannot grow.
