Ocean field notes

02 / 12 · First layer · 0–200 m

An ecosystem powered by one star

Life runs on light

The Surface Layer is bursting with life because it has everything living things need—sunlight, oxygen, and plenty of food. From tiny plankton to enormous whales, many marine species depend on this layer to survive.

Tiny organism, huge job

≈ 50%

of the oxygen we breathe is produced by phytoplankton.

01

Meet the producers

Microscopic powerhouse

Phytoplankton

They use sunlight to make food. Although microscopic, they are among the most important organisms on Earth and begin many marine food chains.

Underwater neighbourhood

Seaweed & kelp

These marine plants provide food, shelter, and safe breeding grounds for fish and many other sea creatures.

02

Who lives up here?

🐟

Fish

Tuna, sardines, and clownfish gather where food is plentiful.

🐬

Dolphins

They hunt in groups and communicate with clicks and whistles.

🦈

Sharks

Top predators help keep fish populations in balance.

🐢

Sea turtles

They eat jellyfish, algae, and seagrass, then surface to breathe.

🐋

Whales

Many visit the surface to breathe and feed before diving deeper.

🪼

Jellyfish

They drift with currents and catch tiny prey with their tentacles.
