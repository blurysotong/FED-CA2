Welcome to the Abyss

Below 4,000 metres, the last trace of sunlight is long gone. Water presses in from every direction, the temperature hovers just above freezing, and the seafloor disappears into a darkness larger than any landscape on land.

Its name comes from the Greek abyssos, meaning “bottomless.” We know its depth now. We still do not know everything moving through it.

Environmental warning

The pressure leaves nothing unchanged

At 4,000–6,000 metres, pressure is roughly 400–600 times greater than at the surface. Deep currents still carry dissolved oxygen here, but no sunlight means no local photosynthesis.

02°

Near-freezing. Always.

Temperature barely changes through the year, usually hovering just a few degrees above freezing.

The weight of the ocean

Soft bodies and pressure-stable chemistry help residents function without being crushed by the water above.

Most of the ocean has no sky

Abyssal depths account for roughly 83% of the ocean’s area. Darkness is not the exception here. It is the dominant habitat.
