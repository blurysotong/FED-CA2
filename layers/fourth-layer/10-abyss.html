The abyssal zone

The quiet floor

Far below the sunlit ocean, broad abyssal plains stretch across the seafloor. The water here is cold, dark, and usually very still, yet small animals continue to find food and shelter.

Abyss at a glance

Depth
Abyssal plains are commonly found between 4,000 and 6,000 metres below the surface.
Conditions
Sunlight does not reach this depth. Temperatures stay near freezing, and the pressure is immense.
Food
Many animals depend on tiny particles that drift down from the ocean above.
