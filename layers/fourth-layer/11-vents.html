Hydrothermal vents

Energy from the Earth

Life without sunlight

At hydrothermal vents, seawater moves through cracks in the ocean crust, heats up, and returns carrying minerals. Around these openings, living communities grow in complete darkness.

Vents at a glance

The source
Cold seawater meets hot rock beneath the seafloor and rises again through chimney-like openings.
The process
Microbes use chemicals from vent water to make food. This process is called chemosynthesis.
The community
Tube worms, clams, crabs, and other animals can depend on these microbes for energy.
