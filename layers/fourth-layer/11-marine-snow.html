Everything down here waits for something to fall

Dinner falls from the sky

The abyss lives on marine snow: the remains of plankton, waste, and organic fragments sinking out of the living world above.

Most of it is consumed before it reaches the bottom. What survives the fall feeds scavengers and microbes waiting in the sediment below.

Survival protocol

Designed to waste nothing

When meals are rare, patience becomes a superpower. Abyssal animals save energy, avoid unnecessary movement, and make every feeding opportunity count.

↘

Slow metabolism

Lower energy use stretches each scarce meal much further.

◌

Pressure-ready bodies

Flexible tissues and specialised molecules keep cells working under pressure.

✦

Living light

Bioluminescence can attract food, communicate, or confuse a predator.

●

Hide in plain darkness

Transparent, red, or black bodies become difficult to spot without sunlight.

✦

Heat in the dark

Where hydrothermal chemistry is available, microbes use chemosynthesis to make food without sunlight. These vent communities are remarkable local exceptions—not the source of energy for the whole abyss.
