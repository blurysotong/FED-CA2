Explore with care

Much of the deep ocean has never been closely observed. Modern submersibles and underwater robots help researchers study it while keeping people safe and disturbance low.

Research at a glance

The tools
Crewed submersibles, remotely operated vehicles, and autonomous robots can carry cameras and sensors.
The approach
Responsible teams observe first, take only needed samples, and plan routes that protect fragile habitats.
The frontier
New maps and shared data can reveal species, landforms, and ocean processes that are still unknown.
